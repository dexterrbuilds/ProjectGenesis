## Release scope

This is an append-only, user-authorized evidence-synthesis successor to **v0.1.0**. It adds reviewed, scoped claims from four verified finalized research packages, a restrictive capability view, an observation-aware sensory-boundary concept and a Stage-5 readiness assessment. ## Evidence categories remain separate

1. **BIOLOGICAL FACT:** preparation-scoped anatomical or experimental observations.
2. **EXPERIMENTALLY SUPPORTED COMPUTATIONAL APPROXIMATION:** only the independently evaluated approximation and its stated validation domain.
3. **HYPOTHESIS:** a proposed interpretation/operator lacking sufficient evidence.
4. **ENGINEERING ASSUMPTION:** a model boundary or specification/governance choice; no physiological identity follows from implementation.
5. **GENESIS PRODUCT MAPPING:** a digital-world interpretation, never physiological evidence.

Negative outputs of an assumed model are recorded as results about that computational scope. Their engineering-category entries do not assert that the observed failure was merely assumed, nor that real flies lack the relevant physiology. Negative, partial and untested conclusions are first-class evidence; no “latest result wins” capability promotion is permitted.

## Nervous-system architecture and identity

The existing C. elegans implementation is unchanged. Drosophila work remains isolated research; this release specifies no unified Genesis fly brain. Anatomical connectivity, physiological operators, local states, plastic state and observation quantities remain distinct.

One biological root identity may own local states where independently justified; no universal representation is selected. APL local-state constraints do not establish nonzero coupling or generalize to all cell types. Unknown physiological sign, receptor effect, normalization, time constant and observation conversion remain explicit unknown/null, not zero.

Canonical anatomy ownership remains exclusively with the existing referenced registry: 5,480 neuron identities and 449,600 aggregate anatomical rows in the historical union. These are aggregate pair/neuropil rows, not invented individual synapse identities. The **4,622 KC→MBON07** and **1,053 KC→MBON11** shared rows remain single canonical identities. Their conflicting Stage-1/Stage-3 update rules remain non-composable. No averaging, rule selection or independent duplicate owner is introduced.

`CANONICAL_IDENTITY_REFERENCES.json` points to those artifacts. Inherited relative object paths in the registry resolve against **the historical v0.1 directory**, stated explicitly in `RELEASE.json`; they are not copied into v0.2. Stage-4 anatomy is cited as experiment evidence only. No new root, row, receptor map or operator is silently admitted into the canonical identity registry.

## Sensory-boundary architecture

A future research adapter may declare observation type, units/time, preparation, joint support domain, immutable provenance, uncertainty, identity resolution and OOD status. This lets an empirical interface state precisely what it measures without claiming to simulate omitted biology. The concept and machine-readable metadata are in `SENSORY_BOUNDARY_CONCEPT.md` and `SENSORY_BOUNDARY_CONTRACT.json`; no adapter is implemented.

For the frozen empirical T4/T5 interface:

- **DIRECTLY MEASURED:** exact source-recording/full-condition retrieval, not predictive validation.
- **INTERPOLATION-SUPPORTED:** only the frozen passing source-tested one-axis brackets.
- **WEAKLY SUPPORTED:** conditional class templates remain weak for individual transfer even when comparison criteria pass.
- **OUT OF DISTRIBUTION / UNKNOWN:** no supported requested response; null, never a fabricated zero waveform. UNKNOWN status dominates any secondary weak-support diagnostic tag.

Output is mean baseline-subtracted **somatic voltage in mV over 0–500 ms**, with documented sampling/resampling. There is no identified conversion to calcium, spikes, release, synaptic current or model rate. Fly, subtype, column and root transfer are prohibited where the independent crosswalk is missing. All **16 Stage-4 physical conditions remain OOD** under the frozen interface; the static audit cannot authorize an LPLC2 evaluation or repair its result.

The empirical catalog is not a photoreceptor→lamina→medulla→T4/T5 simulation. Weakly supported estimates cannot be presented as measured physiology, and measured somatic voltage cannot be relabeled an action signal.

## Governance and future evidence

The unchanged v0.1 revision validator validates the 11 additions and entry-specific review records. It retains historical sections and accepts only scoped appended claims. v0.2 adds restrictive sidecar checks for capability promotion, unknown-to-zero conversions, unsupported identity transfer, expanded support domains, duplicate canonical ownership and product-to-biology leakage.

Future experiment declarations must pin the **v0.2 registry SHA and individual dependency hashes**. They cannot override evidence or add result claims in their manifest. A later admission must append independently justified evidence with explicit old ID/hash/scope supersession, separate review and a new immutable release. A source type called `independent_evidence` in the inherited validator means newly admitted evidence, not an assertion of independent biological subjects or replications.

Validators check declared structure, hashes, scopes and review records. They cannot certify the truth of prose, discover undeclared semantic leakage, or independently peer-review a model. Hashes establish integrity, not correctness. Publishing this release grants no experiment or runtime authorization.
