# Biological rationale and unresolved physiology

- [Li et al., eLife 62576](https://elifesciences.org/articles/62576): mushroom-body
  circuitry includes compartment-local and inter-compartment recurrent routes,
  MBON/DAN feedback, APL/DPM and downstream convergence. This motivates restoring
  reciprocal partners and MB-associated neuropils, not inventing effective currents.
  Hemibrain functional architecture is not exact FlyWire root identity.
- [Schlegel et al., 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07686-5): systematic
  FlyWire cell annotations support class/neuropil-informed selection. Generic added
  cell types receive no behavioral labels here. Original stages retain their crosswalks.
- [Dorkenwald et al., 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y): whole
  adult female chemical anatomy motivates a full reference. Published headline
  totals need not equal this pinned file's counting conventions; measure the file.
- [Shiu et al., 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07763-9): simplified
  whole-brain simulation is an established computational approach, not evidence that
  full context automatically supplies motivation or learning. Our rate benchmark is
  not a reproduction of their LIF model or behavioral validation.
- [Sayin et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6839618/): the candidate
  persistence/withdrawal circuit has complex MBON interactions. Added anatomy cannot
  resolve unknown receptor effects, acquisition transduction or absent body feedback.

BIOLOGICAL FACT: observed chemical contacts and audited annotation identities.
COMPUTATIONAL MODEL: closure rules, threshold/gain/sign assumptions, resource boundary,
rate dynamics, normalization, silent omitted inputs and temporal discretization.
GENESIS MAPPING: none.

No peptide/receptor graph is inferred from names or counts. In particular, DPM's
GABA/serotonin/peptide cotransmission and glutamatergic target effects are unresolved.
All such anatomy remains counted even where conservative fast efficacy is zero.
Licensing remains the frozen-source caveat: Zenodo CC-BY4.0, FlyWire guidance
CC-BY-NC4.0, annotation licensing unresolved; local research only, no deployment.

## Mechanism versus topology: additional primary-source check

[Hattori et al., 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5806120/) links
odor-specific α′3 response suppression to the compartment's odor-evoked dopamine.
[Pribbenow et al., 2022](https://elifesciences.org/articles/80445) identifies
postsynaptic nicotinic-receptor contributions. These constrain mechanism hypotheses;
adding chemical contacts does not specify receptor-state kinetics or their gain.
A small numerical effect does not refute those experiments, and high anatomical
retention cannot validate an arbitrary scalar approximation of their physiology.

The [open full text of Shiu et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11446845/)
provides an important counterexample to dismissing full-brain models on size alone:
a connectome/transmitter-based LIF model yielded experimentally testable sensorimotor
predictions. This study measures a different rate operator and does not import that
paper's feeding/grooming interpretations or claim to reproduce its validation.
