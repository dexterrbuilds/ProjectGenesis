# Limited reward-comparison assay

Recorded before running this assay. It cannot establish orthogonal state axes in
one brain: the frozen α1 preparation and additive α′3 preparation are separate.
A full joint factorial would require a newly justified combined model and is outside
this experiment. In particular, absence of PAM11 from α′3 is NOT evidence that
reward never affects biological familiarity.

Use 119 common PN roots; seed2701 selects two disjoint, equal 6-root patterns from
the sorted intersection. Both models receive identical PN currents. Counterbalance
A/B. Factor E: 0 vs 10 one-second pre-exposures separated by six-second gaps (70 s
phase in both arms). Factor R: both arms receive the same 60-second cue; only the
reward arm of the frozen Stage-1 model receives its established 1 s PAM11 current
on alternating seconds. Finish with 10 seconds silent. No novel/unseen claim is
made after the 60-second stimulus. Stage 2 NEVER receives an imposed DA current.

Read baseline and final rates from independently restored snapshots, probe1 s,
mean last0.5 s. Save both models' plastic snapshots and genuine activity traces.
Report α1 response depression, α′3 response depression, unseen-cue changes and
E×R interaction numerically. Any zero Stage-2 R interaction is imposed by the
separate preparation, not a biological finding. The useful comparison is whether
unreinforced exposure and appetitive teaching modify different local plastic
states in their respective preparations; joint biological interaction is unresolved.
