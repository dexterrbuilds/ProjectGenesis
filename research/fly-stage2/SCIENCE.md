# Scientific separation

## BIOLOGICAL FACT

This experiment uses real directed chemical-synapse contacts and annotated neuron
identities from adult female FAFB/FlyWire v783, with v3.1.0 annotations. The functional
α′3 familiarity evidence and cross-specimen identity crosswalk are cited in
[PLAN.md](PLAN.md). Exact functional identity of the ambiguous partner cells, precise
contact compartment locations and physiological strengths are not established.

## COMPUTATIONAL MODEL

Dimensionless rates, class gains, contact normalization, silent boundaries, a local
PPL104-dependent efficacy rule, contact-specific state, and exponential recovery
are explicit approximations. None were measured from this EM specimen. The
model is not a molecular, spiking, whole-fly or behavioral reconstruction.

The evidence supports a tiny persistent change within this model, selectively
abolished by its learning-mechanism controls. Its magnitude and biological
interpretability are insufficient for the prospective Stage-2 claim. The separate
reward assay cannot determine same-brain reward–familiarity interactions.

## GENESIS MAPPING

**None.** No novelty score or behavior is supplied to Genesis. The model accepts
sensory current vectors; presentation history can affect only persisted neural
and efficacy state. It has no external cue-memory store.

See [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) for the PARTIAL-INCONCLUSIVE result and quantitative
limits. Stage 1 and canonical Genesis are preserved. No Stage 3 is authorized.
