## Crosswalk confidence

The pinned type/root crosswalk in `../fly-physiology-study/CROSSWALK.json` remains unchanged. Stage roots, including MBON07/11, are string identifiers; source annotation hash and all three extraction hashes are recorded there. Current work adds **no** inferred hemibrain-to-FlyWire root equivalences. MBON14/alpha3 measurements remain a separate type-level comparison; alpha-prime3 is distinct. KC alpha-beta-core measurements are not assigned to all KC roots. Functional MB compartments are finer than the MB_VL/ML labels in the aggregate anatomy table. Driver membership, contact location, receptor identity and physiological equivalence remain separate assertions.

## Access and source-quality audit

Source workbooks retain their original bytes; published figures/first rows were known where stated in PLAN.md. APL Figure3 challenge and MBON supplement were extracted only after FIT_FROZEN.json. The MBON article, current figures/source link and indexed full text were rechecked; no authoritative correction or cell-level provenance resolving the discrepancy was located. Search failure is not proof no correction exists. No author was contacted. Keep this dataset's diagnostic calculation, but exclude it from clean validation and model selection.

Dryad metadata was accessible for Prisco; workbook browser/API downloads failed. Zenodo5904586 is code, not the missing numerical workbook. JHU returned403. Stahl's linked Dryad API returned an identifier access/error message even with HTTP200. These are access limitations, not evidence that published datasets never existed. No figures were silently digitized or error bars treated as independent observations.

**BIOLOGICAL FACT:** the published measurements and anatomical identities. **COMPUTATIONAL MODEL:** conditional fits, compatibility proofs, benchmark equations and identity schema. **GENESIS MAPPING:** none.
