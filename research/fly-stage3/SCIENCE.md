# Stage 3 scientific limits

The exact prior conclusions remain Stage 1 PASS (narrow appetitive associative
learning) and Stage 2 PARTIAL-INCONCLUSIVE. Stage 3 cannot overwrite either result.

The literature supports state-dependent MB computations but not an unconditional
mapping from single-neuron firing to persistence decisions. The slow resource
variable here is a computational body-boundary state, not starvation, financial
runway or motivation supplied as an answer. Its only direct neural target is the
PPL101 pathway (except the explicitly invalid global-gain negative control).

No peptide source/receptor connection is inferred from cell names or edge counts.
The model tests a directional phenomenology; dose-response curves, co-transmission,
receptor localization, physiology of omitted inputs and a complete motor readout
remain unresolved. MBON11/MBON18 relation and LH aliases require particular caution.

Anatomical contact counts constrain activity and local modulation but do not specify
synaptic strengths. Body-state gain, efficacy dynamics and the OA response are
model assumptions. A reduced output after an imposed OA pulse is not itself proof
of autonomous disengagement. No cue causing zero activity is not proof of choosing
to abstain. Long decay, global gain and maintained-cue responses are separately
audited and cannot independently satisfy the prospective criteria in PLAN.md.

The report distinguishes raw neural effects from unsupported behavior claims.
