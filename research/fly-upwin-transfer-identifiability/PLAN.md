Preservation hashes may cover other packages, but their scientific results are not inputs. No new literature acquisition, neural simulation, Stage-1 rerun, physical parameter fitting, action decoder, or Stage-5 protocol.

Before analysis, pin releases and source hashes and verify protected bytes. Keep separate observation domains for Stage-1 model state, α1 conditioning spikes, R64A11 acute voltage, R64A11 population calcium, SS67249 recall voltage, SS33917/SS33918 behavioral assays, and individual FAFB roots.

For each quantity distinguish the finite observed statistic from a population or mechanistic estimand. Classify IDENTIFIED / BOUNDED / QUALITATIVELY CONSTRAINED / NON-IDENTIFIABLE, with assumptions and scope. No probability interval based on independent cells is accepted as a population bound when fly clustering and sampling identity are unknown.

Use minimal nested transfer hypotheses and algebraic observational-equivalence counterexamples. No numerical neural trajectory is simulated. Arbitrary normalized numbers used to verify identities are mathematical witnesses, never biological parameter estimates. Do not fit model complexity or coefficients to Stage-1 effects. No latent nuisance value may be exported as a measured biological quantity.

Challenge sign, monotonicity, amplitude, time scale, saturation, mixture fraction, and mediation. Preserve finite observations exactly while changing unobserved transfer properties where possible. For every IDENTIFIED claim attempt a same-observation counterexample and narrow/downgrade the claim if one exists.

Evaluate design sufficiency separately from a calibrated or biologically validated pathway. State which conclusions would hold across the entire evidence-admissible family, which require explicit unverified restrictions, and which are impossible because the family includes null/reversed/alternative-input mechanisms. Outline governance requirements for uncertainty/blinding only; do not specify a Stage-5 experiment, network, stimuli, parameter grid or acceptance threshold.
