# Independent physiology and data audit

Reviewed 17–18 September 2026. All experiments below concern **adult D. melanogaster** unless explicitly excluded. FAFB v783 is one adult female EM specimen, not the animal used for these physiological recordings. A population/type match is not a root-specific physiological measurement. “Unresolved” means the relevant preparation metadata or numerical files were not verified here; it does not mean the information cannot exist.

## Data access and exclusions

- **Acquired intact:** Amin Figure 4 and Figure 7 source XLSX, SHA-256 pinned. Only Figure 4D fits; Figure 7 remains unfit supporting spatial data, not a second selected-success fit. Numerical source values are preserved including negative fluorescence changes.
- **Acquired for local reading only:** primary PDFs Hige/Hattori, publisher HTML Amin/Pribbenow/Tsao, Europe PMC XML Sayin/Barnstedt/Hafez. No redistribution of copyrighted full text in the research deliverable. Their hashes/URLs are recorded in `SOURCE_ACCESS.json`.
- **Located, not converted to a kernel fit:** Dryad calyx data; JHU MBON14 repository. These have incompatible or missing observation/stimulation mappings to the frozen synthetic-cue kernels. Existence of a dataset does not validate cross-cell-type transfer.
- **Not acquired as individual-trial numerical data:** Hige paired currents, Hattori recovery, Handler timing, Sayin physiology. Published aggregate constraints remain useful; raw data are not invented or digitized without declared error.
- Pribbenow's linked code addresses connectivity, not an audited numerical fluorescence dataset. Article availability is not raw-data availability.
- Europe PMC requests for Hige, Gruntman, Hattori and Handler XML returned server errors; primary PDFs or indexed primary text were used where available. These failures do not establish absence of underlying data.
- No mammalian eligibility trace, locust time constant, larval synapse rule, or another paper's fitted model parameter is promoted to adult FAFB physiology.

## Category separation

**BIOLOGICAL FACT:** measured quantities above, preparation metadata, transmitter/receptor interventions and the pinned anatomical identities.

**COMPUTATIONAL MODEL:** fluorescence transfer fit, integrated-depression surrogate, normalization schemes, rate equations and identification arguments. Their assumptions are explicit in `IDENTIFIABILITY.md`.

**GENESIS MAPPING:** none. There is no money, semantic novelty, task value, action decision, LLM input or product objective in fitting.
