## Questions fixed before numerical extraction

1. Which transformations discard physical luminance, contrast polarity, spatial extent, temporal structure, or local input identity? Distinguish absent signals, an uncertain coordinate correspondence, operator exclusions and observation uncertainty.
2. Does a strictly polarity-exclusive two-site correlator represent the measured responses of T4/T5 to isolated bars/flashes? Its algebraic null predictions can be tested without any gain/tau search or an LPLC2 network.
3. Does independent LPLC2 physiology require a nonlinear **observable**, a nonlinear neuron, or separately dynamic dendritic compartments? These are distinct hypotheses. Super-additive axonal calcium alone does not localize a membrane nonlinearity.
4. Which omitted inputs carry physiologically distinct information? Analyze anatomy and known functions without adding neurons.

## Data/assay selection

Primary quantitative candidate: the independent Gou/Matulis/Clark visual-sparsity dataset, Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.t1g1jwtbs. Select the published Figure-7 isolated moving-bar velocity sweep, both T4 and T5, every supplied speed and both contrast polarities. Follow the authors' unit/ROI and response-window conventions after inspecting the supplied documentation/code. Do not choose conditions by effect size. No looming/expansion data are involved.

If fly identity and response units can be audited, compare two **observation-level** families on the per-fly responses averaged across all supplied speeds: (P) a preferred-polarity mean with non-preferred response fixed to zero, and (C) a separate mean for each polarity. Fit sample means only to the independent biology. Use leave-one-fly-out prediction, reporting errors on both polarities, paired differences and the extra parameter. This comparison can reject the strict-polarity restriction; it cannot identify synaptic gain, membrane kinetics, contrast-adaptation rules or a full T4/T5 neural model. No physiological kernel is frozen from a two-mean fit. If fly identity/units are unresolved, do not call ROI folds independent flies; downgrade or omit fitting.

Additional datasets: Gruntman et al. 2021 T4/T5 composite receptive-field recordings (Figshare collection 5629663); Ramos-Traslosheros/Silies 2021 ON/OFF spatial response data; Zhao et al. 2025 eye map; Pang et al. 2025 L1/L2 temporal responses. Audit availability, sizes, preparation, versions and crosswalks. A listed but inaccessible or unprocessed dataset must not be called validated. Large raw archives need not be downloaded merely to claim completeness when a directly relevant public processed dataset is available.

If raw quantitative data cannot be accessed, retain published quantitative constraints with their approximate precision. Do not digitize decorative curves or manufacture standard errors. Analytical comparisons against structural zero/additivity restrictions remain possible, but do not claim held-out numerical validation without actual measurements.
